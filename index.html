    Getting lean, honestly

    You can't burn fat from one spot. Crunches build your abs, but the fat on top comes off from your whole body over time. That's why every day mixes core, legs, arms and a cardio finisher.

    Food and sleep do most of the work. Eat mostly whole foods, get protein at each meal, drink plenty of water, and aim for 7–9 hours of sleep. Walking more on top of this helps a lot.

    Form beats speed. Slow, controlled reps work the muscle better and keep your joints safe. If something hurts sharply, stop and use the easier version.

    Move up a level when the current one feels easy for a full week. Feeling sore for a day or two is normal; pain in joints is not. If you have a health condition or are pregnant, check with a doctor first.
